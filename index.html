I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Philosophy at Syracuse University.

My research focuses on metaphysics, especially truthmaking, ontological commitment, and higher-order metaphysics. My dissertation develops and defends a truthmaker-based account of ontological commitment while proposing a novel theory of truthmaking that makes use of sui generis higher-order quantification.

Before coming to Syracuse, I received an M.A. and a B.A. in philosophy from Yonsei University.

About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Philosophy at Syracuse University. My research focuses on metaphysics, especially truthmaking, ontological commitment, and higher-order metaphysics.

My dissertation develops and defends a form of truthmaker monism. I also work on questions concerning quantification, grounding, modality, and metaphysical explanation.

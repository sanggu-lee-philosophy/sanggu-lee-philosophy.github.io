I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Philosophy at Syracuse University. Before coming to Syracuse, I received an M.A. and a B.A. in philosophy from Yonsei University.

My research focuses on metaphysics and its intersections with philosophy of language and philosophical logic. My dissertation develops and defends a truthmaker-based account of ontological commitment and a theory of truthmaking that makes use of sui generis higher-order quantification. My broader philosophical interests include general philosophy of science, philosophy of action, and philosophy of AI.
